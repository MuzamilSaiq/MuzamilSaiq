## toy-to-theory

A series of pedagogical notebooks that build each idea twice: once as a toy small enough to hold in your head, then again as a working implementation. Written for people who want to know why a method works, not just how to call it.

- **[toy-to-theory-inventory-flow](https://github.com/MuzamilSaiq/toy-to-theory-inventory-flow)** — Queueing theory and Little's Law (L = λW), from a marble-polishing toy to bay-level dwell-time estimation and warehouse flow classification.
- **[toy-to-theory-naive-bayes](https://github.com/MuzamilSaiq/toy-to-theory-naive-bayes)** — Conditional independence and classifier design, from coin flips to a full categorical Naive Bayes built from scratch.
- **[toy-to-theory-bag-of-words](https://github.com/MuzamilSaiq/toy-to-theory-bag-of-words)** — Text vectorization from tokenization through feature vectors, in pure Python, benchmarked against `CountVectorizer`.

## Applied analysis

- **[us-cancer-incidence-trends](https://github.com/MuzamilSaiq/us-cancer-incidence-trends)** — Age-adjusted incidence versus total case burden in CDC/NCI registry data, 1999–2022, with an executive Power BI summary.
- **[afghanistan-covid-model](https://github.com/MuzamilSaiq/afghanistan-covid-model)** — Descriptive analysis of early pandemic reporting under severe data constraints; negative binomial diagnostics against a smoothing baseline.
- **[geospatial-twitter](https://github.com/MuzamilSaiq/geospatial-twitter)** — Spatiotemporal modeling of 14M geotagged tweets across a 1° latitude–longitude grid.
